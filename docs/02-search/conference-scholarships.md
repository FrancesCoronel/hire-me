# Conference Scholarships {docsify-ignore}

> Conferences are a great way to learn, meet people and find your next role. These tech conferences offer diversity travel grants or scholarships.

Most of them require applicants to be at least 18 and a member of a group underrepresented in tech.

This list used to live in [DiversifyMe](https://github.com/FrancesCoronel/diversify-me). Thanks to everyone who contributed there!
